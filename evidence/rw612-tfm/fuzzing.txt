## Run 1: aegis_proto.c 0.2.0 (as reviewed), 4 workers
Every worker stopped within seconds on the seed rng-fails-at-sign (PQKEMINIT, then a PQKEMINIT whose signature
fails for want of randomness, then the PQKEMFIN of the first exchange):
  ORACLE: PQKEMFIN without an exchange: ERR cred other-key
The refused PQKEMINIT had left the older exchange pending with its own key and nonce in it. Fixed in 0.2.1
(a valid PQKEMINIT cancels the pending exchange and wipes its decapsulation key first).

## Run 2: 0.2.1, 8 workers, 120 s
No finding.

## Run 3: 0.2.1, 8 workers, 30 min, corpus from runs 2 (and the inputs below)
Stopped at once on "ORACLE: PQHB reply": a harness error, not a device error. Operation 7 with an empty payload set
an empty status text, which the partition never produces (its status always starts "requests="); the heartbeat reply
then had an empty field. The harness now keeps the status non-empty, as on the board.
